SECTION 17: ROUTE COMBINATION RULES
===================================

1. A route from A to B can be combined with a route from B to C only if the endpoint and starting point refer to the same verified physical location.

2. Never assume that a route can be reversed without checking whether the pathway is bidirectional or whether entrances, stairs, or one-way restrictions affect travel.

3. Do not combine two routes merely because they mention the same block. The exact entrance, floor, or junction must match.

4. When a user gives a starting location, select that location as the route origin.

5. When no starting location is given, use the Main Gate.

6. When the destination is inside a building, include the required floor and the final internal connection.

7. If a route includes an unverified connection, identify it internally and avoid presenting the entire route as fully confirmed.

8. When several valid routes exist, choose a suitable verified route and provide alternatives only when they are known.

9. Attach a campus photograph to a route step when an image is available for the relevant landmark. Missing photographs must not prevent directions from being provided.

10. Keep route instructions short and numbered. Each step should describe one movement or decision.
